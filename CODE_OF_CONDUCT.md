# Code of conduct

Dhuan is a public-health project built by volunteers. Everyone who takes part,
in issues, pull requests, discussions or the data itself, is expected to:

- Be respectful. Disagree with ideas, not people. No harassment, no personal
  attacks, no discriminatory language, no unwelcome attention of any kind.
- Be honest with data. Every number in this project is dated and sourced;
  keep it that way. Do not present estimates as measurements or a detection
  as a verified fire.
- Be careful with people. Do not post anyone's personal information, and do
  not add layers that watch individuals (the edition keeps cameras and
  military layers out on purpose).
- Assume good faith, and say so when you are unsure rather than guessing.

Maintainers may edit, remove or decline contributions and comments that break
these expectations, and may block repeat offenders. To report a problem,
open an issue or contact the maintainer through their GitHub profile
([@sattyamjjain](https://github.com/sattyamjjain)); reports are handled
privately and taken seriously.
